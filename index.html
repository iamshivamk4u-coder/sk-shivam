Most learners spend 3–5 hours a week. Every lesson is available on-demand, so you can move at your own pace.

Do I get a certificate?+

Yes. Complete your projects and receive a shareable SkillLance certificate for your profile and portfolio.

Can I get help if I’m stuck?+

Absolutely. Ask in the community, attend office hours, or get guidance from your mentor.
